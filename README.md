**VICE HEAT: Port Soleil** — a complete original 3D open-world crime game. Rex Marlowe is back in Port Soleil for one last job: drive the sun-soaked coastal city, steal cars, outrun the port police, and finish five story missions — from a quiet pickup at the docks to the yacht at the marina. Traffic and pedestrians, a wanted level with pursuing police cruisers and foot cops, combat, a HUD with minimap, start screen, pause, busted/wasted endings and a localStorage save. Synthesized WebAudio sound and music, no audio files. Built with Three.js, vendored under game/vendor/.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Drive with W A S D, look with the mouse, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, E to enter or leave a car, click to shoot, P to pause, M to mute.
